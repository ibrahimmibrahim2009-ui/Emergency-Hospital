# MedStar Emregency Hospital
## A Web platform designed to simplify emergency medical management, where it quite helps the medical teams handle difficult emergency cases efficiently. This responsive website facilitates access to emergency contacts and staff availability. It also informs the patients and administration with the available capacity of emergency services such as the varient prices of some surgeries.

## Responsivity
### The website is fully responsive depending on grid layout at different device screens.

# The Main Sections
## Header 
### Emergency hotline number, its name, primary action buttons which are Contact Now, Get Appointment
## Info
### Where a brief information about the emergency foundation and its services.
## Stats
### Includes the most important statistics about the hospital such as years of experiencs etc.
## Contact 
### It introduce quick contact button and choice for learn more if you want to know more about set up.
## Services
## Different services and medical surgeries hospital provide for its patients.
## Prices
### Offer categories for some medical sugeries, including brief info about each.
## Doctors
### Well-known doctors the hospital feels proud of them and make patients more comfortable with the procedures.
## Blogs
### Some blog posts the hospital provide for spreading awareness among people with the needed advice from professional docotrs.
## Form
### This is the form every patient has to fill for booking his appointment, and this is not for dangerous emergencies.
